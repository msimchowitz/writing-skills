# Temporary writing revision plan

- Preserve the distinction between the two human-facing documents:
  - `human-writing-guide` remains a manual for people.
  - `human-vs-agent-writing` becomes an exposition in the author's research
    voice, not a guide, instruction, or exhortation.
- Infer abstract practice from the example papers and encode it in the
  `abstract-writing` skill. Abstracts should help readers understand the
  argument rather than summarize the paper section by section.
- Revise the abstracts in both human-facing documents with that principle.
- Research English prose cadence and repeated contrast forms, including
  constructions such as "treats X like Y, not Z." Encode the findings in a
  `writing-cadence` skill, connect it to Humanizer, and apply it to
  `human-vs-agent-writing`.
- Humanize the full `human-vs-agent-writing` exposition, especially its
  staccato runs, uniform sentence lengths, guide-like commands, and repeated
  contrast templates.
- Add a `better-usage` skill for manually curated examples of words that do
  and do not make literal sense together. Its central repair is to name a
  missing intermediate when changing the verb alone remains sloppy.
- Add a compact `academic-voice` skill that identifies the writer as a
  thoughtful, careful, precise academic who cares deeply about making the
  material clear and easy for the reader to understand. Preserve personality
  and purpose while excluding corporate, LinkedIn, Reddit, and GitHub speak.
  Apply this voice to the `human-vs-agent-writing` exposition.
- Fix semantically mismatched verbs in Humanizer. Claims do not stop, and
  "conditions limit the claim" is still imprecise. Prefer relations such as
  "which conditions limit the extent of the claim" or, when asking about a
  theorem's implications, "Where do the consequences of the theorem end?"
  Preserve "the limitations of the claim being made" only where it accurately
  names limitations rather than a process performed by the claim.
- Require `paper-writing`, `general-writing`, and Humanizer to inspect
  abstracts as a distinct part of the artifact, not only body prose.
- Build and inspect both documents, validate the skills and repository, then
  review the final diff for voice, cadence, and accidental scope changes.
- Remove the worked-example chapter from the human writing guide and update
  the guide's source map before the final build.

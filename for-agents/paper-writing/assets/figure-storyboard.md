## Figure Audit

- [ ] The figure answers one scientific question.
- [ ] The visual hierarchy matches the argumentative hierarchy.
- [ ] Method colors match every other figure.
- [ ] Labels are legible at final publication size.
- [ ] Axes, units, sample counts, and uncertainty are defined.
- [ ] Baselines use comparable data, initialization, and tuning, or the
      difference is explicit.
- [ ] The caption states the result without overclaiming the mechanism.
- [ ] Qualitative examples state selection procedure and include failures when
      decision-relevant.
- [ ] The surrounding paragraph explains why the figure changes the argument.

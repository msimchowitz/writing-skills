\section{Final manuscript check}
\label{app:checklist}

\subsection{Argument and evidence}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,label=\(\square\)]
    \item The problem, gap, central claim, and boundary are explicit.
    \item The motivation begins with the scientific problem and its actual
    bottleneck, not with the nearest acronym.
    \item Every abstract claim appears in the main paper.
    \item Every introduction contribution points to visible evidence.
    \item Each mechanism claim has evidence that bears on mechanism.
    \item Hypotheses, planned tests, expected outcomes, and validated findings
    are labeled separately.
    \item Comparators, metrics, settings, budgets, and uncertainty are named.
    \item Limitations narrow claims where those claims first appear.
    \item The conclusion introduces no new result.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Structure and prose}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,label=\(\square\)]
    \item Each section helps the intended reader answer one question.
    \item Each paragraph helps the reader follow one inference.
    \item Every sentence gives the reader a needed claim, condition,
    explanation, or transition.
    \item Each structural rule explains the understanding or decision it
    supports.
    \item The title and every section or subsection heading accurately
    describe the material that follows.
    \item Captions and other display labels state the evidence or action they
    frame.
    \item Main subjects and verbs are easy to find.
    \item Opening clauses supply a real condition, cause, or contrast.
    \item Quantitative statements report values and settings before
    interpretation.
    \item Evidence verbs match the strength of the evidence.
    \item Technical terms, method names, and notation remain consistent.
    \item Specialized methods are explained for the intended audience before
    an acronym carries the argument.
    \item Distinctive borrowed language and framing are cited.
    \item The final edit preserves the authors' voice and calibrated
    uncertainty.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Figures and source}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,label=\(\square\)]
    \item Figure~1 communicates the problem, contribution, and main evidence.
    \item Every later figure answers one question left open by Figure~1.
    \item Captions and surrounding prose make the same claim.
    \item Labels, legends, and annotations are legible at final size.
    \item The top-level entry point records the manuscript and appendix order
    clearly.
    \item Venue files are unmodified and every supported mode builds.
    \item TODOs, comments, revision colors, and undefined references are gone.
    \item The clean source bundle includes every figure and bibliography file.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Rendered PDF}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,label=\(\square\)]
    \item Every page has been inspected at normal zoom.
    \item No text, table, equation, or figure crosses a margin.
    \item Floats appear near the prose that interprets them.
    \item Links, footnotes, page numbers, and appendix references work.
    \item The paper reads naturally at speaking pace.
\end{itemize}

This guide is for researchers turning technical evidence into a paper that
readers can follow and evaluate. It helps authors decide what the evidence
supports, then organize and revise the manuscript around that claim without
erasing technical limits or the authors' voice. Use it while planning a paper
and again when a draft needs structural revision. The final checklist provides
a separate standard for submission review. A careful reader should finish
with a clear sense of the central claim, the evidence behind it, and the point
where the claim stops.

\section{Revision as constrained inference}
\label{sec:revision}

Style revision is safest when it proceeds from the document's evidence and
reader model toward its sentences. The order matters because a local
improvement can quietly change a factual claim, while a broad cleanup can
erase the cadence that made the passage recognizably the writer's.

\subsection{The source defines the admissible claims}

A conservative revision begins with a model of the whole draft, including its
central point and intended reader. The source's factual boundary and recurring
voice then constrain the possible rewrites. Calibrated uncertainty belongs in
this protected set because changing ``suggests'' to ``shows'' changes the claim
even when the sentence becomes shorter.

\subsection{Paragraphs expose the hierarchy of ideas}

Clusters of formulaic language often reveal a paragraph whose governing
relation is missing. At this scale, revision identifies the decisive claim and
groups evidence according to the role it plays. Evidence for the result should
remain distinct from qualifications that fix its extent and from mechanistic
details that explain why it may hold. Their order should make those
dependencies recoverable without assigning every sentence the same visual or
rhythmic weight.

\subsection{Sentences expose agency and relation}

Sentence-level revision locates the grammatical subject and verb, then tests
the logical role of each modifier. This is where an unnamed actor, a mechanical
relative clause, or a mismatched verb becomes visible. Cadence enters at the
same scale, although the relevant pattern usually spans several sentences
rather than one.

\subsection{Comparison with the source completes the inference}

The revised passage is not finished until it is compared with the source.
That comparison can recover a specific detail, qualification, or abrupt
transition lost during cleanup. It also separates chat residue and decorative
endings from genuine voice. Reading at speaking pace makes repeated clause
shapes audible, but the source remains the authority on which irregularities
belong to the writer.

\subsection{A compact example}

\agentdraft

Effective writing is not just about clarity; it is about fostering connection,
enhancing engagement, and ensuring that ideas resonate. By leveraging a
reader-centered approach, writers can create robust communication that truly
makes an impact.

\humanrevision

Effective writing gives a particular reader the information needed to
understand or use an idea.

The first draft combines a canned contrast with a forced group of three.
Promotional language and a corporate verb add ceremony to a simple claim,
while the reference to readers never identifies their need. The revision
states that need directly.

This degree of compression would be wrong if the paragraph contained evidence,
scope, or a mechanism. It works here because the discarded phrases repeat one
unsupported abstraction. Revision can remove that repetition without
establishing a general preference for one-sentence paragraphs.

The limiting case is an edit so regular that the writer disappears. A
successful revision still contains the writer's facts and judgment, and its
cadence remains recognizable even where another editor would have chosen a
different sentence.

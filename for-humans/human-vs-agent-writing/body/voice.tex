\section{Voice}
\label{sec:voice}

Voice is often mistaken for ornament or conspicuous personality. In strong
human writing, voice begins earlier. It appears in what the writer notices,
which tension remains unresolved, and how much space a particular exception
receives. These choices show a mind attending to a subject rather than a style
applied uniformly to content.

\subsection{Specific details constrain generic prose}

Names, measurements, dates, odd quotations, and concrete observations narrow
the range of plausible continuations. They also give the reader something to
verify. Agent prose often replaces them with broad claims of significance; an
overzealous humanizer can repeat the loss by replacing every irregularity with
smooth, generic language.

Specificity does not license invention. A detail belongs because it comes from
the writer's experience or the available evidence, not because vivid prose
sounds more human. The same constraint applies to every factual and technical
claim.

\subsection{Human judgment can remain mixed}

People do not always arrive at balanced conclusions. A writer may consider a
policy broadly useful and still distrust one consequence; a study may show a
strong result in one regime alongside an unexplained failure in another. When
the evidence does not settle the tension, preserving it is more accurate than
extracting a clean lesson.

Agent prose often resolves this discomfort through symmetry. An optimistic
final paragraph softens criticism, uncertainty becomes a balanced caveat, and
an unexplained observation becomes a broad takeaway. The resulting passage
sounds complete because its tone is settled, not because its evidence is.

\subsection{Borrowed registers conceal the writer}

The generic agent register draws from several recognizable sources. Corporate
language turns ideas into objects of alignment, impact, or ownership. LinkedIn
cadence produces one-line lessons and motivational closure. Reddit supplies
fake-casual throat clearing; GitHub contributes terse inventory prose and diff
narration. Chat adds praise for the question and offers to continue.

The human sentence sounds more academic without relying on formality for its
own sake. It names the distinction and explains why it matters. The agent
sentence could describe almost any writing tool, which is precisely why it
reveals so little about this one.

\subsection{Genre shapes the expression of voice}

An essay can support an openly personal voice. Technical or legal prose may
require neutrality, yet neutrality need not become generic. A careful academic
still reveals priorities through exact comparisons and calibrated uncertainty.
The decision to pause over one exception also carries voice.

Deliberate irregularity belongs to this account of voice. A parenthetical
aside, abrupt transition, or distinctive word can carry information when the
reader can infer its purpose. Random variation does not become human merely by
being uneven. The difference is whether the choice reflects a judgment the
writer can explain.

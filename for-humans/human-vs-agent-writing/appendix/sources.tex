\section{Sources and acknowledgments}
\label{app:sources}

This guide distills two agent-facing resources in the
\href{https://github.com/msimchowitz/writing-skills}{writing-skills}
repository: Humanizer version 2.13.0 and the general-writing skill.

Humanizer builds on Siqi Chen's
\href{https://github.com/blader/humanizer}{humanizer}, which draws its pattern
catalog from Wikipedia's
\href{https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing}{Signs of AI
writing}. The general-writing framework builds on Peter Yang's
\href{https://github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop}{no-ai-slop}. Both upstream
projects are distributed under the MIT License, and their license notices
remain with the corresponding skills.

The causal hypotheses in \cref{sec:hypotheses} are conjectures presented for
discussion and testing. They should not be attributed to the upstream
projects.

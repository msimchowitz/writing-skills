\section{Revision checklist}
\label{app:checklist}

Use this checklist after the substantive edit. A failed item calls for another
pass, not a justification for the current sentence.

\subsection{Meaning and voice}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,label=\(\square\)]
    \item Every factual claim, example, statistic, quotation, and opinion comes
    from the source or the writer.
    \item Calibrated uncertainty and technical distinctions remain intact.
    \item Distinctive vocabulary, cadence, humor, tension, and useful asides
    still sound like the writer.
    \item Strong sentences remain unchanged when they already serve the
    reader.
    \item The amount of cutting matches the actual problem.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Reader and clarity}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,label=\(\square\)]
    \item The intended reader and the reader's use for the document are clear.
    \item Every paragraph gives the reader a needed claim, condition, example,
    transition, or moment of voice.
    \item Concrete facts survive, and unsupported abstractions have not gained
    invented detail.
    \item Main subjects and verbs are easy to locate.
    \item Head nouns retain the right focus across relative clauses, and
    responsible actors are named when agency matters.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Language and structure}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,label=\(\square\)]
    \item Importance claims, promotional adjectives, vague attribution, and
    decorative analysis have been replaced with supported information.
    \item Filler, stacked hedges, fake-strong verbs, and abstract wrappers have
    been removed when a direct phrase preserves the meaning.
    \item Question-word wrappers have become direct nouns or statements unless
    the question is genuinely unresolved.
    \item Lists represent real sets, and paragraph structure shows priority
    rather than surface completeness.
    \item Headings support navigation; bold labels, decorative emoji, and
    chat residue do not substitute for hierarchy.
    \item Sentence lengths and paragraph openings vary without stacked
    punchlines or mechanical symmetry.
    \item Stable objects keep stable names instead of cycling through
    synonyms.
    \item The conclusion ends on a supported result, limit, decision, or next
    action.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Final read}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,label=\(\square\)]
    \item The revision sounds natural when read to a sharp colleague.
    \item Quoted language, titles, proper names, and technical terms remain
    unchanged.
    \item Punctuation and capitalization match the writer, genre, and venue.
    \item The document contains no claim about agent authorship based only on
    style.
    \item The writer would recognize the facts, judgment, and voice as their
    own.
\end{itemize}

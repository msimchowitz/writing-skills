\section{Revision checklist}
\label{app:checklist}

Use this checklist after the substantive edit. A failed item calls for another
pass, not a justification for the current sentence.

\subsection{Meaning and voice}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,label=\(\square\)]
    \item Every factual claim and example comes from the source or the writer.
    Statistics, quotations, and opinions do too.
    \item Calibrated uncertainty and technical distinctions remain intact.
    \item Distinctive vocabulary and cadence still sound like the writer.
    Preserve the writer's humor, tension, and useful asides as well.
    \item Strong sentences remain unchanged when they already serve the
    reader.
    \item The amount of cutting matches the actual problem.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Reader and clarity}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,label=\(\square\)]
    \item The intended reader and the reader's use for the document are clear.
    \item Every paragraph gives the reader a needed claim, condition, example,
    transition, or moment of voice.
    \item Concrete facts survive, and unsupported abstractions have not gained
    invented detail.
    \item Main subjects and verbs are easy to locate.
    \item Relative clauses and passive constructions sound natural in the full
    sentence. Responsible actors are named when agency matters.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Language and structure}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,label=\(\square\)]
    \item Unsupported importance claims and promotional adjectives have become
    sourced facts. Treat vague attribution and decorative analysis the same
    way.
    \item Filler and stacked hedges have been removed when a direct phrase
    preserves the meaning. Fake-strong verbs and abstract wrappers have too.
    \item Question-word wrappers have become direct nouns or statements unless
    the question is genuinely unresolved.
    \item Every series has a governing relation. Ordinary paragraphs do not
    stack list-shaped sentences or hide repetition behind paragraph breaks.
    \item The title and every section or subsection heading accurately frame
    the material that follows.
    \item Captions and example labels state the evidence or action they frame.
    Bold labels do not substitute for hierarchy.
    \item Sentence lengths and paragraph openings vary without stacked
    punchlines or mechanical symmetry.
    \item Stable objects keep stable names instead of cycling through
    synonyms.
    \item The conclusion ends on a supported result or limit. When the document
    calls for action, it ends on the decision or next step.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Final read}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,label=\(\square\)]
    \item The revision sounds natural when read to a sharp colleague.
    \item Quoted language and titles remain unchanged. Proper names and
    technical terms do too.
    \item Punctuation and capitalization match the writer, genre, and venue.
    \item The document contains no claim about agent authorship based only on
    style.
    \item The writer would recognize the facts and judgment as their own. The
    voice remains recognizable too.
\end{itemize}
